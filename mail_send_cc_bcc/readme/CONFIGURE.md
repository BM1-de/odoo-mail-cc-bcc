Nothing to configure.

Who gets the one common email:

- every partner in Cc and Bcc, also internal users, and also users who read
  their notifications in Odoo instead of by email: a copy is a copy of the
  email. Who enters himself gets it, too;
- customers and other external partners in To.

A message without Cc and Bcc is only sent as one email if it is a message to
customers (not a notification of Odoo), has at least two external partners in
To and fewer than 50 of them. From that number on Odoo does not show the
recipients of a message to each other, and neither does this module.

The common email has the language and the layout of its first external
recipient in To. It never carries the buttons Odoo shows to internal users.
