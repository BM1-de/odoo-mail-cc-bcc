  to several documents at once (mass mailing mode of the composer) knows the
  Cc of Odoo only and sends one email per recipient.
- The small message box of the chatter has the Cc of Odoo but no Bcc; open
  the full composer.

  the scheduled message does not show them.

  A partner without an email address is left out and marked as failed.

  the recipients of the common email anymore.
