- Cc and Bcc belong to a message on one document. Sending to several documents
  at once (mass mailing mode of the composer) does not offer them.
- The small message box of the chatter has no Cc and Bcc; open the full
  composer.
- The Cc of an email template is added to *To* as in Odoo 19 itself. The
  recipients still get one common email.
- One email has one state: if it is sent, every recipient counts as notified.
  A partner without an email address is left out and marked as failed.
- Followers get their own notification as before. Its header does not name
  the recipients of the common email anymore.
- A message scheduled for later is sent with its Cc and Bcc recipients; the
  form of the scheduled message does not show them.
